## Overview

Chronicle Quill is a personal time capsule application designed to bridge the gap between physical and digital memory preservation. It enables users to scan and digitize handwritten notes, artwork, and physical items, scheduling them for future delivery.

## Goals

The primary goals of Chronicle Quill are to:

* **Preserve Tangible Memories:** Offer a unique way to digitally preserve physical memories.
* **Enhance Future Self Connection:** Facilitate a deeper connection with one's future self through scheduled memory delivery.
* **Provide Secure Digital Storage:** Ensure the privacy and security of users' memories.
* **Create a User-Friendly Experience:** Design an intuitive and accessible platform for memory preservation.

Success criteria are based on user engagement, the variety of memories captured, and the overall user satisfaction with the application.

## Design

The application's design focuses on simplicity and emotional resonance. Key design considerations include:

* **Intuitive Scanning Interface:** A straightforward process for scanning physical items.
* **Clean and Nostalgic UI:** A visually appealing interface that evokes feelings of nostalgia and reflection.
* **Seamless Scheduling:** Easy-to-use delivery date selection for future memories.
* **Responsive Design:** Ensures a consistent experience across mobile and web platforms.

## Development

The development process utilizes a modern tech stack to ensure cross-platform compatibility and performance. Key technologies include:

* **React Native (Mobile):** For building native-like mobile applications for iOS and Android.
* **React (Web):** For creating a responsive web application.
* **OCR (Optical Character Recognition):** To extract text from scanned images.
* **TypeScript:** For enhanced code quality and maintainability.
* **Tailwind CSS (Optional):** For rapid UI development and customization.

## Conclusion

Chronicle Quill provides a unique and personal way to preserve and revisit memories. Its focus on scanning physical items and scheduling future delivery differentiates it from traditional digital journaling applications. By bridging the gap between physical and digital memories, Chronicle Quill helps users create a lasting connection with their past and future selves.
